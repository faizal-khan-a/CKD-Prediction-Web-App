Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) is a long-term condition where the kidneys gradually lose function. Early detection helps prevent complications and slows progression.

⚕️ Key Information

  • Kidneys filter waste and excess fluid from the blood.
  • CKD may cause fatigue, swelling, and changes in urination.
  • Common causes: diabetes, hypertension, and glomerulonephritis.
  • Early detection through blood and urine tests is crucial.
  • Lifestyle and medication can help maintain kidney health.
